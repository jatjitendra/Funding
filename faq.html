FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does the evaluation work? ▼

You trade a simulated demo account and try to hit the profit target for your plan while staying within the max drawdown rules. This is entirely simulated — no real capital is at risk anywhere in this demo.

How fast are payouts? ▼

In this demo, the dashboard shows a static mock payout history to illustrate what a 24-hour payout cadence would look like on a real platform.

What's the profit split? ▼

Most simulated plans on this demo show a 90% split, with Instant Funding plans shown at 80% purely as example numbers.

Is this a real trading platform? ▼

No. ApexFund is a portfolio project built to demonstrate a marketing site, mock checkout, and mock dashboard. There is no real brokerage, capital, or payment processing involved.

Where is my account data stored? ▼

Entirely in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server, and clearing your browser data will remove your demo account.

ApexFund is a portfolio/demo project. All trading, funding, and payouts shown are simulated for demonstration purposes only. This is not a real financial product or offer.
